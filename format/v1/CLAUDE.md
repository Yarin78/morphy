# Note on this directory

These files predate the split between specification and notes described in
[../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md), and they mix the two: layout, evidence, asides and
open questions all run together. They are also older than the v2 work, and some
of what that work settled — how ChessBase compares text when sorting entities,
above all — was guessed at here and is probably wrong.

Until they are rewritten into `v1/spec` and `v1/notes`, treat them as notes:
useful, but not a specification, and not to be quoted as settled.
